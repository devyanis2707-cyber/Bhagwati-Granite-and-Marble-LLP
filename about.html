About Bhagwati

Premium Stone Supplier with a Passion for Excellence

Our Company

Bhagwati Granite and Marble LLP stands as a beacon of quality and reliability in the stone supply industry. With our deep-rooted commitment to excellence, we have established ourselves as a trusted partner for premium granite, marble, and natural stone products.

Our journey is built on the foundation of quality sourcing, fair pricing, and exceptional customer service. We understand that every project is unique, and we're dedicated to providing personalized solutions that exceed expectations.

We take pride in our extensive collection and our ability to cater to diverse requirements - from small residential projects to large-scale commercial needs. Our experienced team ensures that every customer receives expert guidance and support throughout their journey with us.
